GCAF-Net: An Attention-Enhanced Deep CNN Framework with Grad-CAM for Classifying Rare and Endangered Medicinal Plants in Bangladesh

Sadman Rahman*, Saquib Farhan*, Saiba Aabira, Kazi Newaj Faisal

publishedIEEE QPAIN
Read the paper ↗All research

Abstract

The classification of rare and endangered medicinal plants is an urgent necessity in Bangladesh and ecologically similar regions to safeguard biodiversity and conserve traditional medical practices. However, contemporary methodologies are constrained by limited available data and a lack of accurate and interpretable deep learning frameworks. To address these challenges, an advanced convolutional neural network (CNN)- based deep learning architecture named GCAF-Net has been proposed in this paper to identify rare and endangered medicinal plants in Bangladesh. The framework has been designed to combine EfficientNetV2 for feature extraction, a spatial attention mechanism for enhanced feature learning, and Grad-CAM for generating explainable predictions along with a custom CNN model to predict the results. Both global and local features are integrated to achieve improved accuracy. The proposed framework has achieved a macro accuracy of 99.80% and a micro accuracy of 99.71% for the Rare and Endangered Medicinal Plant dataset, establishing new performance standards. The robust performance of the proposed framework is expected to contribute significantly in biodiversity conservation efforts.
