Improved FMCW Radar Sensing for HAR Systems: Synchroextracting Transform-Enhanced Time–Frequency Representation and Custom Anisotropic CNN

Arnob Ghosh, Sadman Rahman*, Labib Siddique Siam, Kazi Newaj Faisal

publishedIEEE Sensors Letters
Read the paper ↗All research

Abstract

Radar-based human activity recognition (HAR) offers noncontact sensing, environmental robustness, and privacy preservation, but its performance is limited by the quality of nonstationary micro-Doppler signatures in frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar signals. Conventional time–frequency methods, such as short-time Fourier transform and Wigner–Ville distribution, suffer from spectral blurring and cross-term interference, reducing sensing resolution. This letter introduces an improved sensor-oriented time–frequency enhancement framework for FMCW radar HAR using the synchroextracting transform (SET), which selectively extracts energy along instantaneous frequency ridges to suppress some artifacts and enhance micro-Doppler concentration. Applied to clutter-suppressed slow-time signals, SET produces highly concentrated and interpretable time– frequency representations (TFRs), directly improving radar sensing fidelity. The proposed approach is evaluated on the NJUST FMCW radar dataset, where SET-derived TFRs show improved concentration and activity separability over conventional methods. Using a lightweight time–frequency anisotropic radar network, the framework achieves 98.71% accuracy and a 96.14% F1-score with low computational cost (0.119 s per sample), demonstrating that sensor-level time–frequency enhancement leads to measurable gains in FMCW radar HAR systems for improved sensor-based performance.
