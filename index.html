§ 01

Hello, I’m Sadman

I’m an M.Sc. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BUET, working with Dr. Shaikh Anowarul Fattah on communication and signal processing. Before that I did my B.Sc. at MIST, finishing 2nd of 97 under Dr. Kazi Newaj Faisal.

I work where signal processing meets AI. A lot of it starts with a messy, non-stationary signal from a sensor on or near the body: I build sharper time–frequency representations of it, then design compact deep networks that learn from them. On the AI side I also build LLM systems, like a local RAG-based peer-review assistant, and I care about models that are accurate, explainable and small enough to live on a wearable.

I’m also a Research Assistant at MIST, building a privacy-preserving fingerprint attendance system for universities in Bangladesh.

Outside the lab I co-founded INSIA Lab, chaired the IEEE MIST Student Branch, and once wrote a peer-reviewed paper about screaming in heavy metal. I’m open to Ph.D. positions.
